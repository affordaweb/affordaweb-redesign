# AffordaWeb Solutions — SEO Audit & Improvement Plan

**Date:** April 3, 2026  
**Auditor:** Cline (Claude Code)  
**Website:** https://www.affordawebsolutions.com

## Executive Summary

The AffordaWeb Solutions website has a **solid technical SEO foundation** with good metadata, structured data, and content structure. However, several improvements are needed to achieve better search rankings:

### ✅ What's Working Well

1. **Technical SEO** — Next.js 15 with App Router, proper canonical URLs, mobile-responsive design
2. **Metadata** — Well-optimized title tags and meta descriptions on all pages
3. **Structured Data** — Comprehensive schema markup (Organization, LocalBusiness, FAQPage, BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList)
4. **Content Quality** — 14 detailed blog posts (1,000+ words each), detailed service pages
5. **Local SEO** — Location-specific pages for NJ, Fresno, Houston, LA, Philadelphia with unique content
6. **Site Speed** — Modern Next.js build with optimized images and caching headers
7. **SSL/HTTPS** — Secure site with proper SSL certificate
8. **XML Sitemap** — Auto-generated sitemap with all important pages

### 🔧 What Needs Improvement

1. **RSS Feed** — Was referenced in robots.txt but didn't exist (now created)
2. **Backlink Profile** — Limited external links pointing to the site
3. **Google Business Profile** — Needs optimization and regular updates
4. **Content Velocity** — Blog publishing has been inconsistent
5. **Internal Linking** — Could be more strategic between related content
6. **Image Optimization** — Some images use external URLs that could be optimized further
7. **Core Web Vitals** — Need monitoring and potential optimization
8. **Search Console** — Must verify and monitor for indexing issues

## Competitor Analysis

### Direct Competitors
1. **Wix** - Strong brand, but template-based
2. **Squarespace** - Beautiful designs, but DIY
3. **GoDaddy** - Cheap, but low quality
4. **Local agencies** - Higher prices, personal service

### Competitive Advantages
- **Price** - $69/month is very competitive
- **All-inclusive** - Design, hosting, SSL, maintenance
- **No contract** - Month-to-month flexibility
- **Professional** - Custom design, not templates
- **SEO-ready** - Built-in optimization

### How to Win
1. Emphasize the "professional design at DIY prices" angle
2. Highlight the all-inclusive nature (no hidden costs)
3. Showcase real client results and testimonials
4. Target local keywords where big players don't compete
5. Create content that addresses common pain points
